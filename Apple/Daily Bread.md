APO BPR Go-Live Announcement

Project Overview
​
We are excited to announce the successful go-live of Daily Bread, our new end-to-end logistics visibility platform, built to drive operational efficiency and power decision-making on OEM shipping and freight execution.

Daily Bread is a centralized intelligence hub designed to unify multi-source logistics and OEM manufacturing data. By consolidating freight capacity, D0 OEM shipments, pack-out plan, finished goods inventory, open orders, and PPS execution metrics, the platform eliminates manual cross-vendor data collection, accelerates freight capacity utilization modeling, and proactively mitigates fulfillment risks from OEM shipping and freight capacity.

One Platform. Full Visibility. Zero Manual Friction.

Go-live Date: 07/31/2026
Annual Savings: 2,853 hrs / year

Key Features
​
OEM Back-dock one stop solution: Highly customizable views and pivot builder for OEM back-dock data visibility. Back-dock including today's shipment data, upcoming pack-out, inventory and open orders.
OEM Shipment data: Automated OEM shipment data and PPS progress data. Covering all FATP sites across China, India, Vietnam, Thailand. Data performance improvement by 30 minutes.
Flight Overview and Back-dock interaction: Converted gross weight and dimension weight by systematic data for flight pre-assessment. Connecting back-dock OEM data to projecting flight utilization at earliest stage to mitigate risk during daily operation.  


Key Wins
​
​End-to-End Visibility: A single, consolidated view of fulfillment status, from pack-out, inventory, to PPS to freight capacity utilization.
​​Data-Driven Agility: Automated reporting gives users customized views for faster, more accurate decision-making.
Operational Excellence: Empowers Logistics to proactively resolve issues, drive down operational efficiency, and elevate customer satisfaction.

项目概览

我们很高兴地宣布，Daily Bread——全新的端到端物流可视化平台——已于近期成功上线（Go-Live）。该平台旨在提升运营效率，并为 OEM（原始设备制造商）出货及货运执行相关的决策提供数据支持。

Daily Bread 是一个集中的物流智能数据中心，旨在整合来自多个数据源的物流及 OEM 制造数据。通过统一汇总货运运力、D0 OEM 出货、Pack-out 计划、成品库存、未结订单以及 PPS 执行指标，该平台减少了跨供应商人工收集数据的工作，加快货运运力利用率建模，并能够主动识别和降低 OEM 出货及货运运力方面的履约风险。

一个平台。全面可视化。零人工操作摩擦。

上线日期： 2026年7月31日
年度节省： 2,853 小时/年
核心功能
OEM Back-dock 一站式解决方案

提供高度可定制的视图和数据透视表（Pivot Builder），实现 OEM Back-dock 数据的一站式可视化。

Back-dock 数据涵盖：

当日出货数据
即将进行的 Pack-out 计划
库存
未结订单（Open Orders）
OEM 出货数据

自动获取 OEM 出货数据及 PPS 进度数据，覆盖中国、印度、越南和泰国的所有 FATP 工厂。

数据处理效率提升 30 分钟。

Flight Overview 与 Back-dock 数据联动

通过系统化的数据，将货物的**毛重（Gross Weight）和体积重（Dimensional Weight）**进行转换，用于航班运力的前置评估。

通过将 Back-dock 中的 OEM 数据与航班利用率预测进行连接，在运营最早阶段即可预估航班运力使用情况，从而在日常运营过程中提前识别并降低潜在风险。

主要成果
端到端可视化（End-to-End Visibility）

通过统一、整合的视图，实现从 Pack-out、库存、PPS 到货运运力利用率的全流程履约状态可视化。

数据驱动的敏捷决策（Data-Driven Agility）

通过自动化报表和可定制的数据视图，帮助用户更快速、更准确地获取信息并进行决策。

卓越运营（Operational Excellence）

赋能物流团队主动识别并解决问题，降低运营成本、提升运营效率，并进一步提高客户满意度。
